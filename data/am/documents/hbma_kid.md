**Date of production:** 30 September 2026.

## What is this product?
**Type:** Shares of a sub-fund of a Luxembourg SICAV qualifying as a UCITS.
**Objectives:** The objective of the fund is to grow the value of your investment over at least four years by investing in a mix of shares and euro investment-grade bonds. The fund holds between 45% and 65% in shares, between 30% and 50% in bonds and up to 10% in cash. The fund is actively managed with reference to the 55% HGERI / 40% HECRI / 5% EUR cash.
**Intended retail investor:** Investors with at least basic knowledge of funds, who can bear losses, and who plan to stay invested for at least 4 years. Minimum initial investment: EUR 500,000.
