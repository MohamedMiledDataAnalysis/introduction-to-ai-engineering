**Date of production:** 30 September 2026.

## What is this product?
**Type:** Shares of a sub-fund of a Luxembourg SICAV qualifying as a UCITS.
**Objectives:** The objective of the fund is to grow the value of your investment over at least five years by investing in shares of companies worldwide. The fund invests at least 95% of its assets in shares. It promotes environmental and social characteristics by excluding companies that derive more than 10% of their revenue from thermal coal and by keeping a weighted average carbon intensity below that of its benchmark. The fund is actively managed with reference to the Harbourline Global Equity Reference Index.
**Intended retail investor:** Investors with at least basic knowledge of funds, who can bear losses, and who plan to stay invested for at least 5 years. Minimum initial investment: EUR 1,000,000.
