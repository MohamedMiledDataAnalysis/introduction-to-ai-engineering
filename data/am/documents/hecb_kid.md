**Date of production:** 30 September 2026.

## What is this product?
**Type:** Shares of a sub-fund of a Luxembourg SICAV qualifying as a UCITS.
**Objectives:** The objective of the fund is to provide income and growth over at least three years by investing in investment-grade corporate bonds denominated in euro. The fund invests at least 90% of its assets in bonds rated BBB- or above. It promotes environmental and social characteristics by excluding issuers that derive more than 10% of their revenue from thermal coal and by keeping a weighted average ESG score above that of its benchmark. The fund is actively managed with reference to the Harbourline Euro Corporate Reference Index.
**Intended retail investor:** Investors with at least basic knowledge of funds, who can bear losses, and who plan to stay invested for at least 3 years. Minimum initial investment: EUR 1,000,000.
